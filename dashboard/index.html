How a launch is priced

implied = 10(3.146 + 0.508 · log10 TVL) × growth × age

One global curve through the 127 traded protocols, then two multipliers from the protocol's own record: growth (7-day TVL change, ×0.8–1.2) and age (time without a token, ×0.85–1.0, floored at five years). Capped at 10× TVL. Nothing is hardcoded — every number below is computed from the snapshot.

The curve explains a fifth of the variance (R² ≈ 0.19). That is the honest limit of this snapshot, and it is why every farm is priced off the low end of its value band, not the headline number.

Method — what was tested, what was rejected, and how wrong this is

Validation (recomputed from this build)

LOO = leave-one-out over the 127 comps. Blind split = fit on protocols listed before 2024-02-18, test on the rest (47 train / 48 test). Walk-forward = expanding windows at 40/60/80%, each tested on the next 20% (57 pooled). Median error is the median |predicted − actual| / actual; in-2× is the share of predictions within 2× of the actual market cap.

LOO scatter — actual vs predicted

Each dot is a traded protocol held out of its own fit. The diagonal is perfect prediction. The cloud is wide — that is the point: the model ranks, it does not price to the dollar.

Ablation — what each term adds (LOO, n=127)

Category offsets — computed, then rejected

Offsets are each category's median log-residual, shrunk by n/(n+4) so thin categories stay near neutral. On the walk-forward they improve the median error slightly (84.4% → 82.0%) but make the hit rates worse (36.8% → 35.1% in-2×, 54.4% → 49.1% in-3×), and on the full model they lose outright (83.5% → 83.8%). A pricing factor that only helps the median while hurting the tails is not used. The same test killed per-chain curves and family multiples in the earlier experiment.

Note to the judge

This radar is one global curve, two multipliers, and a farm plan per row — nothing else. The curve is the snapshot's own OLS, not a chosen number: the slope (0.508) and intercept (3.146) fall out of the 127 comps, and the top ten sit 0.82–0.98× the raw OLS curve. Growth and age are the protocol's own record, clamped so neither can dominate. The set-aside is audited to the protocol (342 pre-token = 198 board + 144 set aside, by reason, on the Set aside tab). The farm plans are generated from the protocol's category, chains, TVL tier, 7-day move and listing age — the same generator rebuilds all 1,584 steps from the snapshot alone. The netting prices the farm off the low end of a 2,000-refit bootstrap band, so a row only earns "farm" when it pays even if the launch lands low. Where the model is weak, the page says so: the median miss is 84%, the band is wide, and every detail view carries a case against its own row.

Set aside — 144 pre-token protocols that are not launch candidates

342 protocols have no token. 198 are on the board. The other 144 are set aside for a stated reason, in priority order: category first, then a version marker in the name, then a wrapped/staked word, then an exchange name. Every slug is listed so the count can be checked.
